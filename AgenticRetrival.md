# Foundry IQ: the future of RAG with knowledge retrieval and AI Search
-[Foundry IQ: the future of RAG with knowledge retrieval and AI Search](https://ignite.microsoft.com/en-US/sessions/BRK196?source=sessions)
-[Slides](https://view.officeapps.live.com/op/view.aspx?src=https%3A%2F%2Fmediusdl.event.microsoft.com%2Fpresentations%2FF5N1%2FBRK196%2FBRK196.pptx%3Fsv%3D2018-03-28%26sr%3Db%26sig%3DyaX5UnOb74T0ih4aWjMYIgOVX3BajmN798h627Ceu%252Fo%253D%26se%3D2025-11-30T09%253A11%253A20Z%26sp%3Dr&wdOrigin=BROWSELINK)
Introduction and Context: The session introduced Foundry IQ, a new AI platform unveiled at Ignite. It aims to empower developers to create intelligent agents capable of accessing, organizing, and interpreting complex enterprise data seamlessly, improving how AI interacts with diverse and detailed information sources.



Evolution of AI Interaction: The speaker highlighted the evolution of AI usage—from carefully crafted queries to more natural, unstructured questions—and explained that Foundry IQ is designed to handle this shift by delivering accurate results even from ambiguous or fragmented inputs.



Knowledge Bases and Sources: Central to Foundry IQ is its “knowledge base” system, which organizes information from multiple sources such as SharePoint, OneLake, Blob Storage, and publicly available data. This structure allows agents to retrieve, process, and synthesize insights without developers managing each source manually.



Integration and Flexibility: Foundry IQ integrates directly with Azure AI Search, supporting both indexed and real-time data retrieval. It offers flexibility for developers to balance speed and depth, enabling optimal performance whether the goal is quick discovery or comprehensive data synthesis.



Security and Governance: Built-in access control, governance, and integration with Microsoft Purview ensure data compliance and protection. Sensitive content is securely managed, with user-level permissions and document labeling enforced automatically during indexing and retrieval.



Performance and Results: Evaluation metrics demonstrate that Foundry IQ’s agentic retrieval delivers significantly higher answer accuracy—up to 36% improvement—over traditional search methods. The system adapts dynamically, optimizing quality against latency for efficient and precise outcomes in enterprise AI scenarios.
